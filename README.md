## About Me

I am an academic pediatric cardiac intensivist and cardiologist at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center and Le Bonheur Children's Hospital.

My clinical work includes the care of critically ill children with congenital and acquired heart disease, mechanical circulatory support, and perioperative management following congenital heart surgery.

I am developing expertise in data science and machine learning, with a particular interest in applying predictive modeling to clinical outcomes research, risk stratification, and decision support in pediatric cardiovascular and critical care medicine.

## Research and Technical Interests

- Clinical machine learning and predictive modeling
- Risk stratification and outcomes after congenital heart surgery
- ECMO and ventricular assist devices
- Electronic health record-based research
- Explainable AI and clinical decision-support systems

## Selected Technical Projects

**Predicting Prolonged ICU Length of Stay — MIMIC-IV**

Data science and machine learning capstone project using approximately 75,760 ICU stays to investigate prediction of prolonged ICU length of stay (≥5 days) using clinical information from the first 24 hours of ICU admission.

**Diabetes Risk Prediction Web Application**

Developed and deployed a machine learning application using Python, scikit-learn, Streamlit, and Render to demonstrate an end-to-end predictive modeling workflow.

[View Live Application](https://streamlit-diabetes-predictor-1.onrender.com/)
